NordVPN vs ExpressVPN: Which One Actually Protects Your IP?

Advertisement

NordVPN and ExpressVPN are consistently ranked among the best VPN services in the world, and for good reason, both have invested heavily in security infrastructure, independent audits, and privacy features. But they take meaningfully different approaches, and the right choice depends on what you value most. This comparison cuts through the marketing and focuses on what actually matters: do they protect your real IP address, and how do they compare on the metrics that affect your day-to-day privacy?

Speed & Performance

ExpressVPN has historically been the speed leader, and its proprietary Lightway protocol, built on the wolfSSL library, is genuinely impressive. Lightway establishes connections faster than WireGuard in many real-world tests, particularly on mobile connections where network conditions change frequently. It also maintains connections more reliably when moving between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

NordVPN closed the gap significantly with the introduction of NordLynx, its WireGuard-based protocol. In most independent speed tests conducted in 2024 and 2025, the difference between the two is marginal on stable broadband connections, typically within 5–10% of each other. Where ExpressVPN's edge is more apparent is on long-distance connections (e.g., connecting from Europe to Asia-Pacific servers) and on mobile devices with variable connectivity.

Winner: ExpressVPN, by a small margin on mobile and long-distance connections. For most users on stable broadband, the difference is imperceptible.

Security Architecture

Both VPNs use AES-256 encryption for their OpenVPN implementations, which is the current industry standard and considered unbreakable with today's computing power. The more interesting differentiation is in their server infrastructure.

ExpressVPN runs its servers in RAM-only mode under a system they call "TrustedServer." This means all server data, including any temporary logs that would ordinarily be written to a hard drive, is stored only in volatile memory that is wiped every time the server restarts. There is literally nothing to seize. This architecture was verified by PwC in their audit.

NordVPN runs collocated servers (they own the hardware or lease dedicated physical servers) rather than renting virtual machines from cloud providers. This reduces the attack surface because there is no hypervisor layer that a cloud provider could potentially access. Their server configuration is also audited, most recently by Deloitte in 2023.

Winner: Tie, both approaches have genuine security merits. ExpressVPN's RAM-only architecture is elegant; NordVPN's physical infrastructure control is also a legitimate security advantage.

No-Logs Policy & Privacy Audits

This is where both providers shine above most of the competition. A no-logs policy means nothing without verification, and both companies have subjected their policies to independent audit.

ExpressVPN has had their no-logs policy audited by PwC multiple times, making it one of the most frequently audited VPN services in the industry. They also demonstrated their no-logs policy in practice in 2017 when Turkish authorities seized an ExpressVPN server as part of a murder investigation and found no useful data on it.

NordVPN was audited by Deloitte in 2023, with auditors given access to the company's systems, infrastructure, and processes. NordVPN also famously had a server compromised in 2018, a serious incident, but the impact was limited precisely because no user data was stored on the server. They have significantly hardened their infrastructure since then.

Winner: ExpressVPN, more frequent audits and a real-world demonstration of the no-logs policy in a high-stakes situation.

Leak Protection

Both providers include DNS leak protection, IPv6 leak protection, and a kill switch across all major platforms. In independent leak tests, both consistently pass without exposing the user's real IP through DNS, WebRTC, or IPv6 channels when configured correctly.

NordVPN's Threat Protection feature adds a layer of malicious domain blocking that can prevent connections to known malware and phishing sites, functioning as a partial ad and malware blocker. ExpressVPN does not have an equivalent feature, though their Lightway protocol's connection reliability arguably reduces the risk of brief exposure during reconnections.

You can test both with our free tool immediately after connecting to verify leak protection is working on your specific device and network.

Winner: NordVPN, for the addition of Threat Protection malware blocking, which adds meaningful security beyond just leak prevention.

Price & Value

NordVPN is significantly cheaper, particularly on longer-term plans. On a 2-year plan, NordVPN typically comes in at around $3.99–$4.99 per month, while ExpressVPN rarely drops below $6.67/month even on their best promotions. Both offer 30-day money-back guarantees.

NordVPN also allows up to 10 simultaneous connections vs ExpressVPN's 8, which matters for households with multiple devices. For families or users with many devices, NordVPN's pricing advantage is amplified.

Winner: NordVPN, meaningfully cheaper with more simultaneous connections.

Ease of Use

Both apps are polished and beginner-friendly. ExpressVPN's interface is arguably simpler, a single connect button with an easily accessible server list. NordVPN's interface is slightly more feature-rich with a map-based server picker, which some users find intuitive and others find cluttered.

On mobile, ExpressVPN's connection reliability edge translates into a smoother experience for users who frequently switch networks. NordVPN's mobile apps have improved substantially in recent versions and are comparable on stable connections.

Winner: Tie, personal preference plays a large role. Both are excellent for beginners.

The Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose NordVPN if: Price is an important factor, you have many devices to protect, you want built-in malware blocking (Threat Protection), or you want the largest server network for the best chance of a fast nearby server.

Choose ExpressVPN if: You prioritise connection speed and reliability above all else (particularly on mobile or when travelling), you value the most frequently audited no-logs policy, or you are in a country where VPN connections are frequently disrupted and need the most robust protocol available.

Both are genuinely excellent choices. The difference between them is small compared to the difference between either of them and a mediocre VPN. Whichever you choose, verify it is working after installation using our free tool.
